# Release 1.2: stadiums 2.0, weather that plays, match texture

Status: scope agreed with the owner on 2026-11-11 (after 1.1.1). Three
workstreams; each lands on its own branch with its own tests and is merged
when green. Match-engine depth (zones, duels, AI manager personality) is
1.3, not here.

## Global constraints

- Never break saves: no schema bump unless purely additive with next-cycle
  markers; old saves load and play.
- Deterministic: anything that touches a result is decided from the fixture,
  career seed and existing inputs only, identically for the shown match and
  the background simulation (see `lib/features/match/match_ground.dart`).
- Calibration: the background `RatingMatchSimulator` and `MatchEngine` guard
  tests stay green; any new effect moves average goals per match by at most
  about 3% across all weathers and must have its own calibration test.
- Copy in all 28 languages via `copy/strings.csv`; no em dashes; never
  "World Cup"; no landmark names in user-facing copy.
- Phone and tablet; goldens re-recorded only where the picture really changed.
- No protected modern landmark is ever added; new landmarks are natural
  features or architecture older than 100 years.

## A. Stadiums 2.0 (visual only, no effect on results)

Today a ground's look is a `StadiumSignature` (kind, roof, crown, bowl,
seats, lights) from the nation code and stadium name, plus a two-layer
backdrop: near layer by region (`backdrop.dart`), far layer by the country's
nature, plus a city landmark for ~50 cities, plus weather.

A1. **City geography.** A curated table of the game's stadium cities (start
with every capital and every city that hosts a national team's home games
after the 1.1.1 rotation) tagging each: coast or harbour, river, mountains
near, desert, forest/park, old town, industrial. The far layer and new near
features follow the city, falling back to today's country rule: harbour
cranes and a lighthouse on the coast, a river with a bridge, snowy peaks for
mountain cities, factory chimneys for industrial towns, old roofs for old
towns.

A2. **The federation's works show.** A ground the manager's federation
built (Arena Nova) gets a modern signature (closed bowl, cantilever or ring
roof, mosaic seats, roof-edge lights). A capacity upgrade adds a tier or a
roof to the existing look. While works on a ground are under way, one stand
shows scaffolding and a crane.

A3. **Small and old grounds have character.** Under roughly 8,000: terraces
behind fences, grass banks, an open end, four corner floodlight pylons, an
old manual scoreboard. 8,000 to 20,000: a single tier with a part roof.
Big grounds keep today's look.

A4. **New public-domain landmarks**, about 25, in cities that have none:
mountains (Ararat, Fuji, Popocatépetl, Cotopaxi, Illimani, Vesuvius, Etna,
Damavand, the Atlas, Kilimanjaro or Mount Kenya, the Alps, a fjord, Lion's
Head) and old architecture (Brandenburg Gate, Cologne Cathedral, Tower
Bridge, Arc de Triomphe, Alhambra, Florence's dome, Hungarian Parliament,
Wawel, Bratislava Castle, Alexander Nevsky Cathedral, Belgrade Fortress,
Gediminas Tower, Narikala, Maiden Tower, Koutoubia, Qutub Minar, Angkor,
Shwedagon, the Great Wall ridge, Teotihuacan, Havana's Capitol). The
existing modern trademarked silhouettes stay in the game but are flagged so
promo and store captures never use them.

## B. Weather that plays

The 2D view already shows rain, heavy rain, snow, fog, heat haze,
thunderstorm, gale, dust storm and frosty nights. In 1.2 the match uses it:

- heavy rain / thunderstorm: shots a little less accurate, long balls and
  crosses less reliable, a few more slips (fouls);
- snow / frosty night: slower tempo, passing a little worse;
- gale: long balls and crosses more erratic, long shots less likely to go in;
- fog: long-range shots worse;
- heat haze: more fatigue (on top of today's heat drain, not double-counted).

The weather a fixture gets must come from ONE deterministic function used by
the 2D view, the shown match and the background simulation. Effects are
small (see the calibration constraint) and are named in the preview's
conditions note and the commentary.

## C. Match texture

- Own goals: rare, from crosses and blocked shots, credited correctly in
  scorers, stats and the timeline.
- Woodwork and big saves become visible moments (pitch view, commentary,
  timeline); the engine already decides woodwork.
- An xG timeline and a momentum graph over the 90 minutes in the stats tab
  (phone and tablet), and on the full-time screen.
