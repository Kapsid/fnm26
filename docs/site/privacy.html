Privacy Policy

Last updated 30 October 2026

FNM collects no data about you. None at all. There is no account to create, no analytics, no advertising, no crash reporting and no tracking of any kind. The app has no server. The only time it uses the network is to check your purchase with the store and, if you turn it on, to back up your saves to your own iCloud.

What stays on your device

Everything. Your saved careers, squads, results, history and settings are stored in a single file inside the app on your phone. They are never uploaded anywhere by the app unless you turn on automatic iCloud backup (see below), and nobody but you can read them.

Backups

You can export your saves to a file and put it wherever you like, including Files, iCloud Drive or Google Drive. That is your copy, in your storage, under your control. If your phone is included in an Apple or Google device backup, the app's data is part of that backup and is covered by Apple's or Google's own privacy policy, not this one.

On iPhone and iPad you can also turn on automatic backup in Settings. The app then copies your saves into its own folder in your iCloud account, so they can be restored on this or another of your devices. The copies go only to your iCloud, never to us, and we cannot see them. Apple stores them under its own privacy policy. Automatic backup is off until you turn it on, and you can turn it off or delete the copies at any time.

Purchases

The one-time Unlimited unlock is sold and processed entirely by the store you bought the app from: Apple's App Store on iPhone, Google Play on Android. The app asks the store whether your account owns the unlock and receives a yes or no. It never sees, handles or stores your name, email address, payment card or any other billing detail. The store's handling of a purchase is covered by Apple's or Google's privacy policy.

Children

The app is suitable for all ages and collects no personal information from anybody, children included.

Third parties

There are none. No advertising networks, no analytics providers, no third-party SDKs that gather data. The only outside parties involved are the store, Apple or Google, for the purchase, and Apple's iCloud if you turn on automatic backup.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the revised version will be posted on this page with a new date above. Since the app collects nothing, any change is likely to be a clarification rather than a new practice.

Contact

Questions about this policy can go to info@footballnationsmanager.com.
