Privacy Policy

FNM, an offline international football management game.

Last updated 23 September 2026

HomeSupport

FNM collects no data about you. None at all. There is no account to create, no analytics, no advertising, no crash reporting and no tracking of any kind. The app has no server, and it makes no network requests of its own.

What stays on your device

Everything. Your saved careers, squads, results, history and settings are stored in a single file inside the app on your phone. They are never uploaded anywhere by the app, and nobody but you can read them.

Backups

You can export your saves to a file and put it wherever you like, including Files or iCloud Drive. That is your copy, in your storage, under your control. If your phone is included in an Apple device backup, the app's data is part of that backup and is covered by Apple's own privacy policy, not this one.

Purchases

The one-time Unlimited unlock is sold and processed entirely by Apple. The app asks the App Store whether this Apple ID owns the unlock and receives a yes or no. It never sees, handles or stores your name, email address, payment card or any other billing detail. Apple's handling of a purchase is covered by Apple's privacy policy.

Children

The app is suitable for all ages and collects no personal information from anybody, children included.

Third parties

There are none. No advertising networks, no analytics providers, no third-party SDKs that gather data. The only outside party involved is Apple, and only for the purchase.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the revised version will be posted on this page with a new date above. Since the app collects nothing, any change is likely to be a clarification rather than a new practice.
