What it is

A whole football world, on your phone

You get one nation and everything that comes with it. The rest of the world plays on whether you are watching or not.

01

Plays offline

Every match, every draw, every transfer window is simulated on the device. No account, no server, no signal needed. On a plane, on the metro, anywhere.

02

Every country in the world

Pick a giant or pick somebody nobody fancies. All of them are there from the first day, and none of them is locked behind a purchase.

03

Endless cycles

Qualifying, your continental championship, the World Championship, then it starts again. Careers run for decades, not for one tournament.

04

Transfers across borders

Your players move between leagues and countries every summer, step up, drop down, and you read about it the same way everyone else does.
