PrivacySupport

Offline by design. No accounts, no ads, no subscription, no tracking, and no network. The whole world is simulated on your phone.

What it is

Every nation in the world, a full player pool that ages, develops and retires, a youth pyramid from the under-13s up, transfers, injuries, press, board expectations and a calendar that keeps turning. You pick one nation and live with the consequences.

Free, and Unlimited

The free game is one complete four-year cycle on two saves, with every nation available and nothing held back. Unlimited is one payment, once, for every cycle after that and as many saves as you want.
